Jacksonville HVAC questions, answered

Quick answers from your local AC and heating technician.

How often should I get my AC and heating tuned up?

Once a year is a good rule. In Jacksonville your AC works hard for much of the year, so a tune-up before summer helps catch small problems early. A heating check in the fall makes sure you are ready for the cooler months.

What are signs my AC needs repair?

Weak airflow, warm air from the vents, strange noises or smells, rooms that never get cool, short cycling, and a sudden jump in your power bill are all common signs. Call us before a small issue turns into a breakdown.

How do I know if I need a replacement instead of a repair?

Age, repair history, and efficiency all matter. If your system is old, keeps breaking down, or is costing more to run each year, replacement can make more sense. We will look at your system and tell you honestly which way to go.

Do you install new AC and heating systems?

Yes. We handle new installs and full system replacements for homes in Jacksonville, for both cooling and heating.

Can you install or replace a thermostat?

Yes. We install thermostats, including swapping an old one for a newer model that fits your system and your routine.
